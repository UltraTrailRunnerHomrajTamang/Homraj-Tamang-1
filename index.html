A life shaped by altitude

More than the miles.
A reason to run.

The mountains were home long before they became a finish line.

Originally from Sindhupalchok, Nepal, I grew up immersed in rural mountain life. Farming, steep trails, and a deep connection to the land taught me resilience, humility, and respect for nature—values that continue to guide my leadership, athletic pursuits, and life in the outdoors.

After more than a decade working in Nepal’s adventure tourism and outdoor industry, one run changed my direction. Inspired by my friend Arjun Rai Kulung, I stood on the starting line of the Kathmandu Marathon in 2019 and completed my first 42 km race. That finish line opened the door to road racing, trail running, ultra-distance events, and a deeper connection to the landscapes I call home.

Today, I bring these experiences together as an endurance athlete who embraces every challenge, an event leader who understands that excellence lies in the details, and a mountain professional who believes the best adventures create meaningful and lasting positive impact for people, communities, and the environment.

From Sindhupalchok to the start line.
